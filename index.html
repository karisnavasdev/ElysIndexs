01 — How it works

Three steps, on repeat.

Everything runs from the trading tax. Holders don't need to do anything but keep the tokens in their wallet.

01

Every trade is taxed

Every buy and sell of $ELYSX routes a 5% fee into the index treasury, paid in HYPE on Elysium.

02

Buy the best of Elysium

Every round, the treasury buys the tokens in the index: the strongest tokens on Elysium, hand-picked and weighted.

03

Airdrop to holders

100% of those tokens are airdropped straight to $ELYSX holders. No staking, no claiming. Hold at least 0.01% of the supply (100,000 $ELYSX) to qualify.

No claimingTokens land in your wallet automatically, every round.
Pro rataYour share follows your balance. Splitting a bag earns nothing extra.
On chainEvery buy and every airdrop is a public transaction.
